I am an AI research engineer working on vision-language models (VLMs) and LLMs. At Technische Hochschule Aschaffenburg I build multi-camera capture setups, VLM-based annotation workflows and evaluation pipelines for KAmA, a BMFTR-funded cognitive AI avatar project.

My background is in automotive engineering (M.Sc. International Automotive Engineering, grade 1.3). My master thesis applied reinforcement learning to autonomous-driving trajectory planning, and I have since worked on robot navigation with ROS2 and Nav2, edge deployment on NVIDIA Jetson, and crash and thermal simulation.

I work mainly in Python, PyTorch and C++, on Linux GPU clusters with Slurm. I speak German (B2), English (C2) and Tamil (C2).
