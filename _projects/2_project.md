- Built a custom OpenAI Gym-style simulation environment in Python and Pygame with configurable traffic and obstacle scenarios.
- Trained and compared Q-learning, DQN and DDPG policies for trajectory planning.
- Designed multi-objective rewards for collision avoidance, lane centering, jerk minimization and speed maintenance.
- Reached an 88% success rate with Q-learning, evaluated on success rate, collision rate and episodic reward, outperforming rule-based and MPC baselines on trajectory-following accuracy.

**Stack:** Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, NumPy, Pygame.
